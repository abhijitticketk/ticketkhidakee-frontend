About the Show

Experience the epic of Mahabharat from a new perspective. "Mahabharat: A New Perspective" brings this timeless saga of Dharma, Karma, and justice to life on a modern stage. This critically acclaimed production stars **Aryan Khan** as Arjun and **Priya Sharma** as Draupadi, with stunning performances from the entire cast that breathe new life into this ancient masterpiece.

Set against a backdrop of contemporary social and political struggles, this adaptation maintains the core essence and philosophy of the Mahabharat while making its themes resonate with today's audiences. The innovative use of multimedia elements and a soulful original score create an immersive theatrical experience you won't soon forget.
