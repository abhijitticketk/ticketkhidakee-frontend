About the Show

"Mahabharat: The Musical" is a grand theatrical adaptation of the timeless Indian epic. Through soulful music and powerful performances, the play explores themes of dharma, destiny, and honor.

Witness the story of the Pandavas and Kauravas unfold on stage, as legendary characters like Krishna, Arjuna, and Draupadi come alive with breathtaking visuals and melodious compositions.

Directed by acclaimed theatre maestro Anil Deshmukh, the production brings together traditional Indian instruments, modern choreography, and a narrative style that appeals to all age groups.
