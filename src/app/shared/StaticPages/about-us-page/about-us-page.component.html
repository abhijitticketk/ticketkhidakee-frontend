About Us

Who We Are

An online ticket booking application for entertainment shows & events. Ticket Khidakee is owned by GLOBILLETE ENTERTAINMENT SOLUTIONS PVT. Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of India.

Our Mission

Through the application, Ticket Khidakee shall provide users with information, pricing, availability, and reservations for booking tickets for entertainment shows, workshops, concerts, and events listed across various cities in India and abroad.

Ticket Khidakee also provides the event organisers with hosting and marketing their events online globally, making it easier than ever to reach your target audience.
